Privacy Policy for YouTube Live Calendar

Last updated: August 26, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how YouTube Live Calendar (hereinafter referred to as the "App") handles information when you use the App.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

The App may access or store the following information.

Google Account Information

When you sign in using Google Sign-In, the App uses account information provided by Google for authentication and account identification.

YouTube Information

When you grant permission, the App uses the YouTube Data API to retrieve information related to YouTube channels registered by you and their scheduled live streams.

Information Stored in the App

The App stores information such as:

Registered YouTube channel information
Live stream schedule information
Notification settings

This information is stored locally on your device for the purpose of providing the App's features.

3. How We Use Information

The information accessed or stored by the App is used for the following purposes:

Providing Google account authentication
Registering and displaying YouTube channels
Retrieving and displaying scheduled live streams
Displaying live stream schedules in a calendar
Providing notifications before scheduled live streams
Providing and improving the App's functionality
4. Data Storage

Information registered in the App is generally stored locally on the user's device.

The operator of the App does not operate a server for storing users' YouTube channel information or live stream schedule information.

5. Google Sign-In and YouTube Data API

The App uses Google Sign-In and the YouTube Data API to provide its features.

Information obtained through Google and YouTube APIs is used only to the extent necessary to provide the functionality of the App.

The use of Google APIs is also subject to Google's applicable policies and terms.

6. Sharing of Information with Third Parties

The App does not sell or share users' personal information with third parties, except where required by applicable law or where necessary to provide services described in this Privacy Policy.

7. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

Google Sign-In
YouTube Data API

The handling of information by these services is governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

8. Deletion of Information

Users can remove registered YouTube channels and related information from the App.

Users can also disconnect their Google account from the App.

Information stored locally on the device may also be removed by uninstalling the App.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as necessary.

If significant changes are made, the updated Privacy Policy will be published at the relevant location.
