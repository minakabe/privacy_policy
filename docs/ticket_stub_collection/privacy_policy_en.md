# Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Ticket Stub Collection respects your privacy.

## Information We Collect

The app does not collect or transmit personal information during normal use.

The following data is stored locally on your device:

* Ticket records
* Exhibition and museum names
* Visit dates
* Notes
* Ticket images
* Favorite settings

## Google Drive Backup

If you choose to use the optional Google Drive backup feature, the app will request permission to access your Google Drive.

The app uploads only your backup ZIP file to your own Google Drive.

The developer cannot access your Google Drive contents.

## Network Usage

The app works completely offline during normal use.

Network communication occurs only when using Google Drive backup or restore.

## Third Parties

The app does not sell or share your personal information with third parties.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when necessary.
