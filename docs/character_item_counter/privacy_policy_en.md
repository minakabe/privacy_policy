# Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

"Character Item Counter" (hereinafter referred to as the "App") respects your privacy and strives to handle information appropriately. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information.

## 1. Information Handled by the App

### 1.1 Information You Register

The App stores the following information that you enter or register only on your device.

* Work information (title, description, currency unit)
* Character information (name, profile, money, notes, tags)
* Item information (inventory; item dictionary entries including name, description, unit price, tags, material / craftable / uncraftable classification, and recipes)
* Records of changes to items and money (type, event name, name of the other party, memo, date and time)
* Images you set for works, characters, and items
* App settings (tutorial completion status and usage slots added by watching ads)

This information is used only to provide the App's features and is never sent to servers operated by the App's developer. The App's developer does not view or collect this information.

### 1.2 Access to Images (Photo Library / Files)

The App accesses your device's photo library (or a file selection dialog on computers) only when you set an image for a work, character, or item.

* Only the image you select is read.
* The selected image is copied to the App's own storage area so that it can be displayed.
* Images are never sent outside your device.
* When you change or remove an image, or delete the work, character, or item it belongs to, the copy saved by the App is also deleted.

## 2. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC, to display rewarded ads (ads that add usage slots when watched). Ads are displayed only when you choose to add usage slots.

AdMob may collect and use information such as advertising ID, device information, IP address, and app usage information for ad delivery and ad performance measurement.

Depending on your consent status, region, and other factors, personalized or non-personalized ads may be displayed.

The information you register in the App (1.1) and your images (1.2) are never sent for advertising purposes.

For information about how Google handles data, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 3. Network Communication

The App's network communication is limited to that required to display ads (Google AdMob).

The App has no features that send the information or images you register (such as cloud backup, sync, or account linking).

## 4. Sharing of Information with Third Parties

The App does not sell or share your information with third parties.

## 5. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party service:

* Google AdMob (Google LLC)

The handling of information by this service is governed by its provider's privacy policy.

## 6. Data Management and Deletion

Information registered in the App is stored only on your device.

* You can delete works, characters, items, records, and images in the App at any time.
* Uninstalling the App deletes all of the App's information stored on your device.
* Because the App has no backup feature, registered information cannot be restored if your device is damaged or lost, or when you change devices.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as features of the App are added or changed.

If there are significant changes, we will notify you through the App or on the page where this Privacy Policy is published.
