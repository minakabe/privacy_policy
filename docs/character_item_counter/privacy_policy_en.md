# Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

"フィクション出納帳" (hereinafter referred to as the "App") respects your privacy and strives to handle information appropriately. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information.

## 1. Information Handled by the App

### 1.1 Information You Register

The App stores the following information that you enter or register on your device.

* Work information (title, description, currency unit)
* Character information (name, profile, money, notes, tags)
* Item information (inventory; item dictionary entries including name, description, unit price, tags, material / craftable / uncraftable classification, and recipes)
* Records of changes to items and money (type, event name, name of the other party, memo, date and time)
* Images you set for works, characters, and items
* App settings (tutorial completion status and usage slots added by watching ads)

This information is used only to provide the App's features and is never sent to servers operated by the App's developer. The App's developer does not view or collect this information.

Only when you back up your data to Google Drive is this information saved to your own Google Drive (see "2. Backup to Google Drive").

### 1.2 Access to Images (Photo Library / Files)

The App accesses your device's photo library (or a file selection dialog on computers) only when you set an image for a work, character, or item.

* Only the image you select is read.
* The selected image is copied to the App's own storage area so that it can be displayed.
* When you change or remove an image, or delete the work, character, or item it belongs to, the copy saved by the App is also deleted.

## 2. Backup to Google Drive

You may optionally use the App's feature to back up and restore data using Google Drive. Only when you use this feature, the App will:

* Sign in to your Google account
* Access your Google Drive

The only Google Drive permission the App requests is the permission to access files created by the App (drive.file). The App does not view or modify any other files in your Google Drive.

The App saves the following as a single backup file (ZIP format) in the "フィクション出納帳" folder in your My Drive:

* The information you registered as described in 1.1 (works, characters, items, records, item dictionary, tags, and usage slots added by watching ads)
* Images you set for works, characters, and items

Backups are saved to your own Google Drive. The App's developer does not view or collect your Google account information or data in your Google Drive, and does not send it to third parties.

You can disconnect your Google account at any time from the App's settings screen. You can delete saved backups from the App's restore screen or from Google Drive.

## 3. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC, to display the following ads:

* Banner ads (at the bottom of the work list, character list, and item dictionary screens)
* Rewarded ads (ads that add usage slots when watched; displayed only when you choose to add usage slots)

AdMob may collect and use information such as advertising ID, device information, IP address, and app usage information for ad delivery and ad performance measurement.

The information you register in the App (1.1) and your images (1.2) are never sent for advertising purposes.

For information about how Google handles data, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 4. Consent for Advertising

The App obtains consent for the use of information for advertising as follows:

* In regions where consent is required by law, such as the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform to obtain your consent. You can change your choices at any time from "Ad consent settings" on the App's settings screen.
* On iOS, the App uses App Tracking Transparency to ask for your permission to track. You can change this at any time in your device settings.

If you do not consent or do not grant permission, the App's features are not restricted. In that case, non-personalized ads may be displayed.

## 5. Network Communication

The App communicates over the network only in the following cases:

* To display ads (Google AdMob) and to check consent for advertising
* To communicate with your Google account and Google Drive when you back up or restore data using Google Drive

## 6. Sharing of Information with Third Parties

The App does not sell or share your information with third parties.

## 7. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

* Google AdMob (Google LLC)
* Google User Messaging Platform (Google LLC)
* Google Sign-In (Google LLC)
* Google Drive API (Google LLC)

The handling of information by these services is governed by their respective providers' privacy policies.

## 8. Data Management and Deletion

* You can delete works, characters, items, records, and images in the App at any time.
* Uninstalling the App deletes all of the App's information stored on your device. Backups saved to Google Drive are not deleted; if you no longer need them, delete them from the App's restore screen or from Google Drive.
* If you have not backed up your data to Google Drive, registered information cannot be restored if your device is damaged or lost, or when you change devices.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as features of the App are added or changed.

If there are significant changes, we will notify you through the App or on the page where this Privacy Policy is published.
