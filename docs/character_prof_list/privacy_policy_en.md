# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

"フィクション名簿帳" (hereinafter referred to as the "App") respects your privacy and strives to handle information appropriately. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information.

## 1. Information Handled by the App

### 1.1 Information You Register

The App stores the following information that you enter or register on your device.

* Work information (title and per-work settings such as how timeline points are managed)
* Character information (name, reading (furigana), content entered in template fields and character-specific fields, tags)
* Template information (template name and fields)
* Tag information
* Timeline records (labels and in-story dates of timeline points, and changes to character settings at each point)
* Images you set for works, characters, and other entries (including images used in timeline records)
* App settings (tutorial completion status, Tips already displayed, sort order of lists, the default way of managing timeline points, the timeline point used as the basis for display, usage slots added by watching ads, etc.)

This information is used only to provide the App's features and is never sent to servers operated by the App's developer. The App's developer does not view or collect this information.

Only when you back up your data to Google Drive is this information saved to your own Google Drive (see "2. Backup to Google Drive").

### 1.2 Access to Images (Photo Library / Files)

The App accesses your device's photo library (or a file selection dialog) only when you set an image for a work, character, or other entry.

* Only the image you select is read.
* The selected image is copied to the App's own storage area so that it can be displayed.
* When you change or remove an image, or delete the work, character, or timeline record it belongs to, any copies saved by the App that are no longer used are also deleted.

### 1.3 Inquiries and Feedback

When you select "お問い合わせ・ご意見" (Inquiries and Feedback) on the App's settings screen, the App opens an input page of Google Forms, a service provided by Google LLC, in your browser.

* The content you enter in the form (the text of your inquiry or feedback, the app it concerns and, if you enter it, your email address for a reply) is delivered to the App's developer through Google Forms only when you submit it.
* When opening the form, the App preselects "フィクション名簿帳" as the app the inquiry concerns. The App does not automatically fill in any other information or submit the form. The information you register in the App (1.1) and your images (1.2) are never sent.
* The content we receive is used only to respond to your inquiry and to improve the App, and is not provided to third parties except as required by law.

### 1.4 JSON Import and Export

The App can import and export information such as works and characters in JSON format. Export formats include a format containing all of the App's information and a format that can be read by the app "フィクション出納帳".

* When importing, the App reads only the text you paste into the input field or the file you select in your device's file selection dialog. The imported content is used only to register works, characters, and other entries in the App. Files are selected through your device's file selection dialog, and the App does not request permission to access your device's entire storage.
* When exporting, the App writes out the information described in 1.1 within the scope you choose (work, characters, or tag). Images are not exported. Timeline records are not exported in the format for "フィクション出納帳".
* The exported content is passed only to the destination you choose (an app or service you select in your device's share menu) or to the clipboard. The App does not automatically send information to other apps.
* Information passed through the share menu is handled in accordance with the policies of the destination app or service. It is never sent to the App's developer.

## 2. Backup to Google Drive

You may optionally use the App's feature to back up and restore data using Google Drive. Only when you use this feature, the App will:

* Sign in to your Google account
* Access your Google Drive

The only Google Drive permission the App requests is the permission to access files created by the App (drive.file). The App does not view or modify any other files in your Google Drive.

The App saves the following in the "フィクション名簿帳" folder in your My Drive:

* A file (JSON format) containing the information you registered as described in 1.1, excluding images and usage slots added by watching ads
* A file (ZIP format) containing the images you set for works, characters, and other entries (not created if there are no images)

Backups are saved to your own Google Drive. The App's developer does not view or collect your Google account information or data in your Google Drive, and does not send it to third parties.

You can disconnect your Google account at any time from the App's settings screen. You can delete saved backups from the App's restore screen or from Google Drive.

## 3. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC, to display the following ads:

* Banner ads (at the bottom of the work list and character list screens)
* Rewarded ads (such as ads that increase the maximum number of works and characters you can manage when watched; displayed only when you choose to watch them)

AdMob may collect and use information such as advertising ID, device information, IP address, and app usage information for ad delivery and ad performance measurement.

The information you register in the App (1.1) and your images (1.2) are never sent for advertising purposes.

For information about how Google handles data, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 4. Consent for Advertising

The App obtains consent for the use of information for advertising as follows:

* In regions where consent is required by law, such as the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform to obtain your consent. You can change your choices at any time from the App's settings screen.
* On iOS, the App uses App Tracking Transparency to ask for your permission to track. You can change this at any time in your device settings.

If you do not consent or do not grant permission, the App's features are not restricted. In that case, non-personalized ads may be displayed.

## 5. Network Communication

The App communicates over the network only in the following cases:

* To display ads (Google AdMob) and to check consent for advertising
* To communicate with your Google account and Google Drive when you back up or restore data using Google Drive

The inquiry and feedback forms (1.3) are opened in your browser, so communication with the forms is performed by your browser. The App itself does not communicate when exporting (1.4), although the destination you choose in the share menu may communicate.

## 6. Sharing of Information with Third Parties

The App does not sell or share your information with third parties.

## 7. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

* Google AdMob (Google LLC)
* Google User Messaging Platform (Google LLC)
* Google Sign-In (Google LLC)
* Google Drive API (Google LLC)
* Google Forms (Google LLC)

The handling of information by these services is governed by their respective providers' privacy policies.

## 8. Data Management and Deletion

* You can delete works, characters, tags, templates, timeline records, and images in the App at any time.
* Uninstalling the App deletes all of the App's information stored on your device. Backups saved to Google Drive are not deleted; if you no longer need them, delete them from the App's restore screen or from Google Drive.
* If you have not backed up your data to Google Drive, registered information cannot be restored if your device is damaged or lost, or when you change devices.

This Privacy Policy may be updated as features of the App are added or changed.

If there are significant changes, we will notify you through the App or on the page where this Privacy Policy is published.
